600 Mora, 400 Mora, and 200 Mora mobs are fought in order by default.
For 200 Mora mobs, they are fought in reverse order of Adventurer Handbook (newest to oldest).
Each monster route is independent, chained together for route optimization.
Individual routes that take 3-5 minutes just to kill a single 200 Mora mob are marked as inefficient routes and can be disabled or deleted manually.

Try to keep the combat rotation within 15-20 seconds.
Team used during recording: Zhongli, Furina, Kazuha, Baizhu (combat strategy provided: Zhongli Furina Kazuha Baizhu.txt).
Auto-loot was turned off during recording (adjust combat config in config group as recommended).
Purpose is to shorten rotation time; most loot is still gathered. Mirror Maidens and Cicin Mages should be placed in separate config groups.

Potential issues:
1. Some Hilichurl camps have two distant Hilichurl Shooters; Furina's summons may not reach them and pull aggro.
Workaround: Trigger a combat cycle slightly outside camp to summon Furina's minions, then sprint towards one shooter, wait for summons to defeat it, then run into center camp.
If your rotation is too long, summons will expire before killing the shooter, getting stuck in combat for 2 minutes.
2. Cryo avatars in pools: monsters are lured out of pools along the route. If investment is too low to break cryo shield, it may get stuck in combat.
Workaround: Execute two combat rotations; if still not defeated, swap team composition or remove the route.
